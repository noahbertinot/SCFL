Collaboration and contact

The program needs people who can test it as much as people who can use it. There are three ways in.

Researchers and grant partners

Independent replication is the program’s most important missing piece. Useful contributions include:

  • running the Stage 2 pairwise-sufficiency test under pre-registration;
  • testing rules for deriving the measurement boundary from the system itself;
  • testing whether results survive legitimate changes of representation;
  • attempting to replicate published readings.

The foundational paper’s synthetic experiments can be reproduced from its supplementary scripts and stated random seeds.

Domain collaborators

Grid operators, hospital systems, insurers, shippers and infrastructure owners who hold data on past events they understand well.

SCFL is read retrospectively on that record, and the reading is compared with outcomes that were not used to build it. Results that fail are reported as failures.

SCFL does not replace the engineering, actuarial, clinical or operational frameworks you already use.

Commercial partners

Organizations that want a structural reading on their own data. A first pilot is one partner and one plate the partner already owns, read in place with no new infrastructure.

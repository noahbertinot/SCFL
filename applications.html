Applications and case studies

SCFL has been applied retrospectively in several domains. Each application below carries its evidence status. None has yet been independently replicated.

How to read these cases

Retrospective

Readings are made on past events whose outcomes are already known. Agreement with a known outcome is not a prediction of the next one.

Labeled

Public facts, the program’s narrative readings and its computed results are kept apart, and each is marked as such.

Alongside, not instead

SCFL does not replace the engineering, actuarial, clinical or operational frameworks used in any of these domains.

Case studies

  • February 2021
    Exploratory

    Texas grid and Winter Storm Uri

    The grid came within minutes of a collapse that could have lasted weeks. The program reads the night as the end of a long structural path, and reports a retrospective reading that held under an adversarial check.

  • March 2011 onward
    Narrative reading

    Fukushima Daiichi

    A cascade across four reactor units and a tail still open. Used to show why a failure is not the same thing as its trigger, and why different parties read the same event on different clocks.

  • In preparation
    In preparation

    Hospital systems

    Case material on hospitals is being prepared with full source citations and will be published here when ready.

Using SCFL in an organization

The program’s proposed delivery model is Measurement as a Service: one measurement layer, read by each party on data it already holds, alongside the forecasts and models it already uses.

SCFL measures the geometry of relational coherence in a system’s state space.

It characterizes how a system’s relationships are organized, how they constrain the configurations the system can occupy, and whether that structure remains recoverable under load. SCFL is a measurement approach. It is not an early warning system.

Measurement, not warning

The distinction is not a matter of terminology. It changes the scientific object being measured.

Early warning systems compared with SCFL
AspectEarly warning systemsSCFL
What it looks atIndicators that a system may be approaching a failure thresholdThe geometric organization of relationships within the system’s state space, including how they constrain the configurations it can take
Primary activityGenerating warningsMeasurement and characterization
PredictionThe defining purposeA possible downstream use, only where separately validated
Place among existing methodsSignals that failure may be approachingMeasures something upstream of performance indicators and failure warnings; complements them rather than competing as another warning system

The difference is between looking for signs of an impending event and characterizing the underlying structure that determines what states a system can occupy and return from. Drift detection, state-transition analysis, recoverability assessment and, possibly, prediction may follow from the measurement. They are not its defining purpose.

The measurement object is proposed, not yet proven. Whether a particular metric succeeds in measuring it is an empirical question, and the program treats it as one.

What SCFL is, and is not

SCFL is

  • A measurement instrumentIt characterizes structural coherence and recoverability.
  • An additional structural layerIt sits above existing data and describes structural properties that operational systems do not report.
  • Read on existing operational dataIt works with data an organization already holds. No new collection is required.
  • Upstream of performance metricsIt characterizes the structure beneath the indicators a system already tracks.

SCFL is not

  • Software you installIt is not a downloadable tool or an on-premises application.
  • A platform replacementIt does not replace existing data systems.
  • A dashboard or risk modelIt adds a structural view alongside them rather than replacing them.
  • An early warning systemIt does not generate alarms or thresholds. It characterizes structural integrity.
  • A new sensor networkIt does not require new instrumentation.

SCFL does not replace established engineering, actuarial, clinical or operational frameworks. It is designed to sit alongside them.

Three structural properties

The working definition is unpacked into three properties. How they combine is itself a testable question, so systems are compared on all three rather than collapsed into one score.

I

Integration

Whether the parts form one relational structure rather than a collection of components. Being connected is not enough.

Km

Mutual constraint

Whether the relationships limit and depend on one another, so that changing one alters what the others can be. Pairwise measures cannot capture this.

Re

Recoverability

The structural capacity to restore organization after perturbation, read from the structure before anyone knows whether recovery occurs.

Where the evidence stands

Results on this site are labeled by evidence status, so it is always clear what has been shown and what has not.

  • Demonstrated (synthetic)

    Pairwise scores are blind to defects in mutual constraint. Across 180 random signed graphs, the frustration index varied at fixed pairwise statistics in 93 percent of conditions.

  • Exploratory

    SCFL has produced structured observations of relational change in electrical grids, cascades, human interior states, and financial, organizational, healthcare and maritime systems. These have not yet been independently replicated.

  • Hypothesis

    Recoverability, estimated by the coherence-capacity vector CCV(t), relates to observed recovery. This has not yet been validated.

  • Planned

    A pre-registered empirical test of whether higher-order structure carries information that pairwise measures miss.

Coherence as a Relational Property: Toward a Foundational Science of Coherence

Ronald L. Brogdon and Juanita Rubio. Position paper and proposed research program. Zenodo.

Gives a working definition of coherence, a three-part construct (integration, mutual constraint, recoverability), a worked example and two synthetic tests, candidate mathematical invariants, ten falsification conditions and a thirteen-step research program. Positions SCFL as the measurement architecture for the construct, not its definition.

Read the paper (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23247214)

All items are deposits on Zenodo, an open research repository. A deposit is not peer review.

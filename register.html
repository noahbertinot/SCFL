Register for the One Question Challenge

A qualifying officer or board member must register. The challenge opens after review.

* Required

For example, Chief Risk Officer or Board Member
Optional at registration. Plain language is fine; the Structural Intake Engine handles the translation.

How registration is sent

Sending opens your email app with your registration filled in, addressed to measurement@coherencemanagement.org. Send that email to complete registration.

Registrations are reviewed before the challenge opens.

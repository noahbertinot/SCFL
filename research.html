Research program

The program does not set out to prove in advance that coherence is general. It sets out to create the conditions under which that claim can fail.

The research sequence

Each phase must be passed before the next is meaningful.

  1. Define

    A candidate object, formalized without circularity.

  2. Measure

    Measurement that can be independently replicated.

  3. Test invariance

    Representation and boundary testing; comparison across domains.

  4. Adjudicate

    Translation between theories; validation or falsification.

A failure at any stage returns the program to the candidate construct, not to a revised score.

Evidence status

Demonstrated results, exploratory analyses, hypotheses and future objectives, kept apart.

  • Demonstrated (formal)

    Mutual constraint is well defined and invisible to pairwise scores. Two structures with identical pairwise statistics can differ in how far they are from holding together. Worked example, foundational paper §4.5.

  • Demonstrated (synthetic)

    Pairwise blindness is typical, not contrived. At fixed pairwise statistics, the frustration index varied in 93 percent of conditions across 180 random signed graphs. Foundational paper §4.6.

  • Demonstrated (synthetic)

    The measurement boundary can dominate the measured value. The choice of box explained 66 to 77 percent of the variance within a system, and a connectivity-based rule for deriving the box failed under tight coupling. Foundational paper §6.4.

  • Exploratory

    SCFL generates structured observations of relational change across domains. Grids, cascades, human interior states, and financial, organizational, healthcare and maritime systems. Not independently replicated outside the program. See applications.

  • Exploratory

    A retrospective Texas grid reading held under an adversarial null test. One event. See the case.

  • Withdrawn

    Four earthquake sequences that looked alike. Found to be an artifact of how fast the event count was changing. Withdrawn and kept in the record.

  • Hypothesis

    Higher-order consistency carries information that pairwise measures miss in measured systems. To be tested in Stage 2 of the first test, below.

  • Hypothesis

    Recoverability predicts observed recovery. Its estimator, CCV(t), has not yet been validated against observed recovery (falsification condition F8).

  • Hypothesis

    Persistence under repeated load requires recoverability to stay above some level. Hypothesis H1, linking the program to constraint-centered theory.

  • Hypothesis

    The same relational object can be recovered from external systems and from human interior states. A risky prediction: changing the substrate should not destroy the object.

  • Planned

    Independent replication, and prediction. Prediction becomes a principal objective only after measurement is established.

Thirteen steps

  1. Fix the phenomenon. Confirm that the observations described by the working definition form a recognizable class.
  2. Separate the construct from its consequences. Keep performance, observed recovery and clinical observation out of the definitions.
  3. Define the components non-circularly.
  4. Resolve the higher-order problem and test the components. Including whether each component is necessary and which combination rule, if any, tracks independent outcomes.
  5. Resolve the problem of the whole. Can the boundary be derived rather than chosen?
  6. Establish measurement that can be independently reproduced.
  7. Test representation invariance.
  8. Test scale.
  9. Compare external and interior domains.
  10. Compare foundational frameworks without manufacturing convergence.
  11. Test downstream manifestations: recovery, performance, clinical observation, resilience.
  12. Test prediction, only after measurement is established.
  13. Attempt falsification. Search actively for conditions under which the construct fails.

The first test: pairwise sufficiency

Stage 1, synthetic. Completed The two probes are separable in practice.

Stage 2, empirical. Planned On existing SCFL representations, compute both probes over matched windows and ask whether the frustration index carries information about later structural change beyond the pairwise score.

Hypotheses, sign rule, windows and decision criteria are to be fixed in a pre-registration before any series is computed.

How the program could fail

The construct is built to be vulnerable. Each condition is paired with the test that could expose it.

What would count as success

These are requirements of the program, not claimed achievements.

  • Non-circularity: each component defined without reference to coherence, its consequences or its detection.
  • Representation invariance: equivalent representations preserve the measured construct.
  • Boundary discipline: the measured whole is not an artifact of the chosen box.
  • Higher-order adequacy: configurations that cannot be reduced to pairs are accounted for.
  • Empirical independence: measurements built without reference to the outcomes they are compared against.
  • Cross-domain recoverability: the construct survives changes of modality and substrate.

Three legitimate outcomes

A general property: a construct that can be defined, measured and recovered across materially different domains.

Partial invariance: some domains share a deeper structure and others do not.

No general object: the commonality is linguistic or domain-specific, and coherence remains a family of specialized constructs.

Each outcome would say something specific: which component failed, which rule fit, or whether the formulation reduces to existing mathematics. All are knowledge.

Limitations and open questions

  • The higher-order probe relocates rather than resolves the circularity of the compatibility judgment, because the sign of each relation must itself be grounded.
  • Recoverability is estimated by CCV(t), which has not been validated against observed recovery.
  • The empirical corpus has not been independently replicated.
  • The boundary problem is demonstrated, not solved.
  • What kind of thing coherence ultimately is remains open.

Measurement as a Service

One measurement layer, read by different parties on their own clocks, on data they already hold. It does not replace the forecast.

What it is

Measurement as a Service is the program’s proposed way to deliver SCFL. A partner’s own data is read through the SCFL measurement layer, in place, without new infrastructure.

Forecasts, catastrophe models, actuarial engines and enterprise risk systems keep doing what they do. The layer underneath reports the state of the structure: where the seams are, how much capacity remains, how the tail is decaying.

The service reports a state. It does not predict.

Status. No commercial deployment is claimed on this site. The Texas grid readings are public structure tests, not live capital tests. The program is seeking its first pilot partners.

Different readers, different clocks

The same cascade looks different from each seat. Each party reads the part it already owns.

  • Infrastructure owner

    Remaining capacity, not a date

    A port or a plant reads the seam on its own telemetry: power, cooling, the wall, the neighbouring unit.

  • Shipper

    The lane can stay on the map while confidence does not

    A shock in one layer, replenishment failing in the next: the strait, the bill of lading, the schedule.

  • Reinsurer

    A closed year first

    A pilot on one book the reinsurer already holds: pricing against realized outcomes, claims against the legal environment, hazard against exposure. If half-life contracts while results are still stationary, that is a recoverability reading. If it does not, the claim fails on that book. The decision stays with the reinsurer.

    The idea of moving from reactive indemnity to continuous risk observation is labeled speculative, not because the measurement is untested but because real-time testing on a book that pays claims is scant.

  • Sovereign or patient-capital fund

    Not the event: the system under the holding

    Whether the system beneath a holding can replenish itself after the ripple has moved.

Terms of a first pilot

  • One partner, one plate. A single dataset the partner already owns.
  • In place. No new infrastructure and no new sensors.
  • A fixed set of operators. Six frozen operators (DRIFT, SHIFT, RUPTURE, KINETIC, QUENCH, STRESS), a closed set rather than a growing vocabulary. Any of them can be refused, plate by plate.
  • Open specification, not a black box.
  • Honest about evidence. A deposit is not peer review.
